Reference site infrastructure

Technical profile of the two studios given as reference points: registrars, DNS, hosting, CDN, and technology stack. Gathered 5 August 2026 from public DNS, WHOIS, and HTTP response headers.

The finding, in one line Both reference sites are Wix. Not similar platforms - the same platform, the same renderer, the same asset CDN. The only meaningful difference is that one pays for a custom domain and the other does not. Every competitive observation in these documents follows from that.

A textbook fully-managed Wix setup: Wix is registrar, DNS operator, CDN and host. The apex resolves to Wix's own 185.230.63.0/24 block and www is delegated into Wix's CDN chain, which terminates on Google Cloud. The absence of MX records confirms the public Gmail address is the studio's only contact route - there is no mail on the domain.

HSTS is set at roughly one year and MIME sniffing is disabled. There is no Content-Security-Policy, no X-Frame-Options and no Referrer-Policy - the site can be framed by anyone. That is Wix's platform default, not a choice the studio made, and it is not something a Wix customer can change. Worth noting as one of the concrete things a custom build does better.

Registration

Registered through Wix on 26 December 2025 - about seven months old, and locked with both clientTransferProhibited and clientUpdateProhibited.

A privacy lesson worth taking The WHOIS record for heckhoundzstudio.com has no privacy protection enabled. The registrant name, organisation, street address and contact details are all publicly readable by anyone running a one-line command - and the address appears to be residential. The specifics are not reproduced here, because publishing an individual's home address into a repository that is going public would be a real harm rather than useful research.

For Starship Suits: enable WHOIS privacy at registration, and prefer a registrar that includes it free for the life of the domain rather than as a paid add-on. For a studio that takes commissions from the public and works from home, this is a safety matter, not a preference. See document 04.

Fur Me Creations - detail

No domain at all. The site lives on a path under Wix's shared wixsite.com domain, which is the free tier, fronted by Cloudflare (server: cloudflare, cf-ray, and a __cf_bm bot-management cookie). Same Thunderbolt renderer and Parastorage asset CDN as Heckhoundz.

The practical consequences of the free tier are worth spelling out, because they are the visible gap between the two studios:

  • The URL is furmecreations.wixsite.com/fur-me-creations - hard to say aloud, hard to put on a business card, and it reads as unbudgeted.
  • No email on the domain, ever. The contact route is a Gmail address and a personal phone number.
  • Search authority accrues to wixsite.com, not to the studio.
  • If they later buy a domain, every existing link, card and social bio breaks.

Notably, this studio is the more commercially diversified of the two - art commissions, merch, deep-cleans and refurbs - and it is still on a free subdomain. The constraint is likely budget or inclination rather than lack of business.

What this means for Starship Suits

ObservationImplication
Both competitors are on Wix templates A custom-built site is an immediate and durable differentiator. In a craft business, the site is itself a work sample - a template says the studio outsources its own presentation.
Neither sets CSP, X-Frame-Options or Referrer-Policy Wix customers cannot set these. A custom site on a real host can, at no cost. Minor for a brochure site, but it is free ground.
Both carry the full Wix Thunderbolt runtime A hand-built static site will be dramatically lighter and faster. That is a genuine advantage on mobile, which is where most of this audience browses.
Heckhoundz uses its registrar as host, DNS and CDN Convenient, but total lock-in. Keeping the registrar separate from the host - as recommended in document 04 - means the studio can change host without touching the domain.
Heckhoundz has no WHOIS privacy Enable it from day one. Non-negotiable for a home-based studio.
Neither has custom email on their domain hello@starshipsuits.com costs a few dollars a month and outclasses a Gmail address on every invoice and enquiry.
Heckhoundz's domain is seven months old There is no entrenched search incumbent here. The category is winnable.

Where Wix is genuinely the better answer

Stated plainly, because the recommendation should not be taken on faith: Wix wins when nobody technical is available to maintain the site. It has a visual editor the studio owner can use without help, hosting and TLS and backups are handled, and a form builder and booking tools come included. Both reference studios chose it, and for a solo maker with no developer that is a defensible choice.

The custom build only wins because there is a developer here. The real question that decides it is in document 04: who edits this site in two years?
